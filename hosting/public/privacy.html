Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Squad HQ ("we", "us") helps amateur football teams manage fixtures, availability, match fees and squad stats. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Name, email address and profile photo, provided by Google or Facebook when you sign in.
  • Phone number, provided by you during onboarding so your team manager can reach you.
  • Team, match and availability data you create or that your team manager shares with you inside the app.

How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account and team membership.
  • To show fixtures, availability, match fees and squad stats to your teammates and team manager.
  • To let your team manager contact you about matches and payment requests.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Your name, avatar and availability are visible to other members of the same team. We do not share your data with third parties outside of the service providers (such as our hosting and authentication providers) needed to run the app.

Data retention

We retain your account and team data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below.
