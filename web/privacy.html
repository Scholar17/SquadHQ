Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026 · Updated 22 October 2026

Squad HQ helps teams and squads organise matches, trips, shared bills and subscriptions, and settle up with each other. This policy explains what information the app collects, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

Squad HQ is run by Thaw Zin Oo. For any privacy question or request, contact scholartzo@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in. When you sign in with Google or Facebook we receive your name, email address and profile photo from them. We never see your Google or Facebook password.
  • Your profile. Your name, photo, phone number and username, which you can change in Profile.
  • What you do in your groups. The teams and squads you join; matches, availability (RSVPs), scores, Man of the Match votes, player ratings and your answers to after-match questions; match bills; expenses and how they're split, including names of guests you add; trips; monthly expenses; comments; and payments you record between members.
  • Venues your team saves. A venue's name, Google Maps link, location (latitude and longitude), photo, notes and price, as an admin enters them. This is the pitch's location, not yours: Squad HQ never reads your phone's location.
  • Payment details you choose to add. A payment QR image, your bank name, account name and account number, and photos of payment slips or receipts. Squad HQ does not process payments or hold money — it only records what members tell it.
  • Photos and the camera. When you add a photo (your profile photo, a payment QR, a slip, a receipt or a venue photo), you choose it from your photos, take it with your camera, or paste one you copied. Squad HQ only reads the photo you choose, take or paste, and only when you ask it to; it doesn't look through your photos or use the camera otherwise.
  • Your device, for notifications. A push notification token and the kind of device (Android, iPhone or web), and a list of the notifications sent to you.

How we use it

Only to run Squad HQ: to sign you in, show your groups their matches, expenses and balances, work out who owes whom, and send you notifications and reminders you can turn off in Profile. We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Members of a team or squad see your name and photo, and what you do in that group (expenses, payments, votes, comments).
  • Your payment QR and bank details are shown to members who pay you back.
  • A payment slip is visible to you and to whoever you paid; a receipt attached to an expense is visible to that squad.
  • Your phone number is part of your profile, which members of your teams and squads can access (the app doesn't display it to them).
  • Shared links. If you share a match or bill link (for example in Messenger), anyone who has the link can see its preview: the team name, the opponent, the kick-off date and time, the venue, the score, and for a bill the name of who paid. Opening the details still needs a Squad HQ account in that group.
  • Otherwise, people outside your groups can't see any of it.

Services we use

We use trusted providers to run the app, who process information on our behalf:

  • Supabase — accounts, the database and file storage (photos, QR codes, slips and receipts).
  • Google Firebase — hosting the web app and delivering push notifications.
  • Google and Facebook — signing you in.
  • Cloudflare — showing the preview of a shared match or bill link.
  • Open-Meteo and the World Air Quality Index project (aqicn.org) — the weather and air-quality forecast for a match. They receive the venue's location only, never anything about you.

How long we keep it

We keep your profile while you have an account. Group history is kept for one year: matches, expenses, comments, slips and notifications are deleted once they're a year old. Debts from deleted expenses are combined into one "carried over" amount so balances stay correct, and a match's bill is kept until everyone has paid. When you delete your account, your personal details are removed as described below.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Profile, tap Delete account, tick the box to acknowledge what's deleted, tap Continue and confirm. It takes effect straight away and can't be undone.

Without the app: email scholartzo@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

What's deleted: your login, name, photo, phone number, username, payment QR, bank details, push notification tokens and notifications, and your membership of every team and squad. A group you own passes to another member; a group only you belong to is deleted.

What's kept: expenses, payments, slips, votes and comments you share with other members stay, shown as "Deleted user", so their balances and history still add up. They are no longer linked to any account or personal details.

Your choices

  • Change your name, photo and phone number, or remove your payment QR and bank details, in Profile at any time.
  • Turn notifications off in Profile or in your phone or browser settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, at scholartzo@gmail.com.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and access rules on our database limit each person to the information of the groups they belong to.

Children

Squad HQ isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
